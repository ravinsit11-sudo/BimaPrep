About insurance

What insurance is, and how each line works

Insurance pools the premiums of many to pay the losses of the few. A contract of insurance is a promise by the insurer to indemnify (general insurance) or to pay a fixed sum (life insurance) on the happening of an uncertain event, in return for a premium.

Six principles every paper tests

Insurable interest

The insured must stand to lose financially if the event happens. In life insurance it must exist at inception; in marine at the time of loss; in fire and motor at both inception and loss.

Utmost good faith (uberrimae fidei)

Both parties must disclose every material fact. Non-disclosure or misrepresentation makes the contract voidable at the option of the insurer, within the limits of Section 45 of the Insurance Act for life policies.

Indemnity

The insured is placed in the same financial position as before the loss, no better. Applies to general insurance; life and personal-accident policies are benefit policies and pay an agreed sum.

Subrogation

After paying a claim the insurer steps into the insured's rights against the third party who caused the loss. A corollary of indemnity, so it does not apply to life insurance.

Contribution

When two or more indemnity policies cover the same loss, each insurer pays its rateable share; the insured cannot recover more than the loss.

Proximate cause

The insurer pays only if the dominant, efficient cause of the loss is an insured peril. Storm blows down a wall that damages stock: proximate cause is storm.

The five lines on this site

Life

Life insurance

Term (pure protection), endowment (death or maturity), whole life, money-back, ULIPs (market-linked, 5-year lock-in) and annuities. Key law: Insurance Act 1938 Sections 38 (assignment), 39 (nomination) and 45 (no policy questioned after 3 years). Grace period 30 days (15 days for monthly mode).

Health

Health insurance

Indemnity plans reimburse hospitalisation; fixed-benefit plans pay a sum per day or per illness. Since the 2024 IRDAI master circular: pre-existing disease waiting period not more than 36 months, moratorium 60 months, cashless authorisation within 1 hour and final discharge authorisation within 3 hours. Portability lets you move insurer with continuity credit.

Motor

Motor insurance

Third-party liability cover is compulsory under Section 146 of the Motor Vehicles Act 1988; own-damage cover is optional. Sum insured is the Insured Declared Value (IDV). No Claim Bonus goes up to 50% after five claim-free years and belongs to the owner, not the vehicle. New private cars carry 3-year TP cover; new two-wheelers 5-year.

Crop

Crop insurance (PMFBY)

Area-yield scheme: farmer pays at most 2% of sum insured for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi and 5% for commercial/horticultural crops; the balance is government subsidy. Threshold yield = average of the best 5 of the last 7 years' yields × indemnity level (70/80/90%). Losses are measured by crop-cutting experiments; localised calamities and post-harvest losses are assessed on individual farms, intimated within 72 hours.

Property

Property and fire insurance

Standard fire policies cover fire, lightning, explosion, aircraft damage, riot/strike/malicious damage, storm, flood, impact damage, subsidence, bush fire and bursting of tanks. Standard products since 2021: Bharat Griha Raksha (homes), Bharat Sookshma Udyam Suraksha (SI up to ₹5 crore) and Bharat Laghu Udyam Suraksha (₹5–50 crore). Under-insurance attracts the average clause.

Glossary

Terms you will be asked

Peril
The cause of loss (fire, flood).
Hazard
A condition that increases the chance or size of loss.
Deductible / excess
First part of every claim borne by the insured.
Co-payment
Fixed percentage of each health claim borne by the insured.
Reinstatement value
Cost of replacing with new, without deduction for depreciation.
Floater
One sum insured shared across several locations or family members.
About the regulator

IRDAI, the Acts, and how a policyholder is protected

What IRDAI is

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India was set up under the IRDA Act 1999 and is headquartered in Hyderabad. It registers insurers and intermediaries, approves products, prescribes solvency (a control level of 150%), protects policyholders and promotes orderly growth of the industry.

The legal frame

Insurance Act 1938
Principal statute: registration, capital, investments, assignment, nomination, Section 45.
IRDA Act 1999
Creates the Authority and its powers.
Motor Vehicles Act 1988
Compulsory third-party insurance (Ch. XI).
Marine Insurance Act 1963
Codifies marine cover and insurable interest at loss.
Public Liability Insurance Act 1991
Compulsory cover for hazardous units.

Policyholder protections (2024 regulations)

Free-look period of 30 days for every policy with a term of one year or more. Insurers must acknowledge a grievance immediately and resolve it within 14 days. Health policies: PED waiting period capped at 36 months, moratorium at 60 months, no claim can be contested after the moratorium except for fraud. Nominee and assignment rights follow Sections 39 and 38 of the Act.

How to complain

1. Insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer. 2. If unresolved in 14 days, IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal or toll-free 155255. 3. Insurance Ombudsman (17 offices) for disputes up to ₹50 lakh; the award is binding on the insurer. 4. Consumer Commission or civil court for other matters.

Who sells insurance

Individual agents (one life, one general, one health insurer; IC-38 exam by III), corporate agents (up to 3 insurers in each line, raised to 9 in 2022), brokers, web aggregators, insurance marketing firms, point-of-sale persons and the insurers' own staff. Banks sell as corporate agents (bancassurance).

Institutions you will meet

III
Insurance Institute of India, Mumbai: Licentiate, Associateship, Fellowship exams.
IIB
Insurance Information Bureau: industry data, motor and health databases.
GI Council / Life Council
Self-regulatory bodies of general and life insurers.
TAC (historic)
Tariff Advisory Committee; tariffs withdrawn from 2007 except motor TP.
Agriculture Insurance Co.
Public-sector crop insurer; PMFBY lead in many states.

Figures above are as notified by IRDAI up to 2024. Regulations change; the exam follows the version in force on the exam date, so re-check the IRDAI website (irdai.gov.in) before you sit.
